FAQ

Is Eat ABQ free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no account or login required.

How are the restaurants picked?

Every restaurant is a real Albuquerque spot rated 3.7★ and up on Google, checked against Google's live data monthly — so closed places drop off and new ones show up automatically.

Does it work without internet?

Mostly — restaurant data lives on your device, so browsing, filtering, and spinning all work offline once you've opened the app at least once.

Is my location or data shared?

No. Location is only ever used on your device and never uploaded. Analytics are anonymous and fully optional, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Can I customize the wheel?

Yes — hide categories you never eat, set default filters for price/distance/rating so every spin already matches your taste, and pick a theme, including Red Chile, Green Chile, and Christmas (both!).

I own a restaurant — can I get featured?

Yes — Eat ABQ offers paid Featured promotions for local restaurants. Email feedback@eatabq.app to ask about it.
